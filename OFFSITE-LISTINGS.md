## Getting ready for a Google Business Profile

1. **Decide how the address is shown.** If Suite 205H is staffed with signage and clients can walk in, list it as a
   location. If not, set the profile up as a service-area business with the address hidden. Showing an unstaffed
   suite is the most common reason new profiles get suspended.
2. **Line up the paperwork under one name.** Business registration or trade name, PUC permit, insurance and a
   utility bill or lease should show VIP Aspen with the same address and phone. Corsa's legal text currently reads
   "Summit Executive Transportation LLC doing business as VIP Aspen".
3. **Prepare proof for video verification.** Real photos of the Cullinan, Escalade and Sprinter in Aspen, branded
   items, and the office if it is shown.
4. **Category and areas.** Primary category "Limousine service". Service areas: Aspen, Snowmass Village, Basalt,
   Woody Creek, Carbondale, Glenwood Springs.
5. **Reviews.** Once live, send the review link after each completed ride. No incentives, and ask every client, not
   only the happy ones.

## Settings only you can change

- **Cloudflare is blocking AI crawlers on the live site.** Measured on 2026-11-24 by requesting the homepage with
  the GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot crawler user-agent strings: each request received "403 Forbidden". The
  robots file Cloudflare adds also disallows Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended and others. To be quoted by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini, turn off "Block AI bots" and the managed robots.txt
  (Cloudflare dashboard, AI Crawl Control / Bots). Allowing training crawlers is a separate choice from allowing
  search crawlers; the search ones matter most.
- **At launch, stop sending search engines to Prerender.io.** The new site already serves full pages. Delete the
  `PRERENDER_TOKEN` secret on the `vipaspen-prerender` Worker (or recache every page in Prerender.io), otherwise
  Google keeps seeing the old design from its cache.
- **Booking and contact forms.** The three `VITE_SUPABASE_*` build variables were missing on the live build when last
  checked, so the contact and inquiry forms fail there.
- **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Submit the sitemap after launch and request indexing of the
  homepage and the main service pages. About 10 of 40 pages were indexed by Google at the last check.

## The 2027 airport closure

Aspen airport is closed from April 4, 2027 (11 p.m.) to November 19, 2027 (7 p.m.) for runway reconstruction, with
no commercial or private flights. Travelers who need a transfer during the closure will arrive through Eagle, Rifle,
Grand Junction, Montrose or Denver. A guide page on getting to Aspen during the closure, plus the listings at
Eagle airport and the FBOs above, is the largest single opportunity found in this review.
